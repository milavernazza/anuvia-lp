DORA methodology

4 metrics. 4 levels. 1 roadmap.

Deploy Frequency

How many production deploys per day/week/month. Elite: multiple per day. High: daily. Medium: weekly. Low: monthly+.

Lead Time for Changes

Time from commit to production. Elite: < 1 day. High: < 1 week. Medium: < 1 month.

Mean Time to Recovery

Average time to recover from a production failure. Elite: < 1h. High: < 1 day. Medium: < 1 week.

Change Failure Rate

% of deploys that cause degradation. Elite: 0-15%. High: 16-30%. Medium: 16-45%.
