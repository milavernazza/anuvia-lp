Frequently asked

FAQ.

How does the 3× ROI guarantee work?+

Typical investment {{ fmt_price('R$ 45-60k') }}. If the annualized savings identified are not at least 3× that (e.g.: {{ fmt_price('R$ 135-180k') }}+/year), you receive a full refund. The criterion is objective: identified savings vs. amount invested. Documented in the contract.

How much access to the AWS account is needed?+

Read-only IAM role with specific policies: Cost Explorer, Trusted Advisor, Compute Optimizer, Billing, Resource Discovery. No write access for our audit. The client can revoke at any time.

Why can you save 25-40%?+

Most AWS-native companies grew their bill faster than their FinOps maturity. Recurring patterns: under-used Reserved Instances / Savings Plans, over-sized compute, invisible data transfer costs, orphaned snapshots/EBS, S3 without lifecycle, third-party SaaS on Marketplace above market. We know where to look.

What if our company is too large / too small?+

Sweet spot is $30k-$300k/month AWS bill. Below $10k/month the savings in absolute value probably won't cover the audit. Above $300k/month the scope is negotiated case by case (8-12 week engagement).

Do you implement the recommendations or only flag them?+

During the audit, in week 3, we execute low-risk quick wins with your approval (typically 10-15% immediate savings). The rest is documented for you to execute — or contract Managed FinOps Retainer for Anuvia to run continuously.
