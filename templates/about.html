Origin

Why Anuvia exists

Anuvia was founded by an ex-AWS Solutions Architect (Brazil), ex-Google and ex-MongoDB, after years of seeing a recurring pattern in consultancy work: the gap between what sales decks promise and what production environments actually accept. Brilliant PoCs in closed rooms. Demos with no rollback. Slides with impeccable architectures — and, the day after contract signature, a delivery short on the operational details that make systems survive.

The choice was to invert the priority. Discovery exists to separate promise from feasibility; PoV exists to validate measured numbers against a held-out set; Build exists to integrate with the client's legacy stack without disrupting it; Operate exists so the system keeps running after the consultant leaves the room. Each phase has explicit gates, and a PoV that misses its eval thresholds returns a no-go report rather than a softened threshold.

Every architecture ships with an Architecture Decision Record. Every deploy ships with a documented rollback procedure. Every workload ships with a measured cost ceiling. It's not the fastest path to revenue, but it's the one that produces clients who renew.

How we think

Four non-negotiable principles

Engineering before marketing

Technical rigor comes first. Proofs of value validate real feasibility before any production commitment. Every impact promise is accompanied by cost calculation and operating plan.

Production standards from the first commit

Integrated observability, security by design, eval harness, documented rollback plan, defined operational ownership. Every project delivered includes these elements — without exception.

Radical transparency

Public prices. Explicit deliverables. Realistic timelines. Recorded SLAs. The client knows before signing what they will receive, in what timeframe, and with which acceptance criteria.

Real operation, not rhetoric

Anuvia operates with the same stack it offers to clients. When we say the system works in production, it's because it has been running here for months. Verifiable demonstrations instead of aspirational slides.

Como pensamos

Quatro princípios não-negociáveis

Engenharia antes de marketing

O rigor técnico vem primeiro. Provas de valor validam viabilidade real antes de qualquer compromisso de produção. Toda promessa de impacto é acompanhada de cálculo de custo e plano de operação.
